Our studio

A space to return to your origin

Origin Massage is a boutique studio born from the union of two therapists with complementary paths: Flor Sánchez, specialist in therapeutic bodywork and Thai techniques, and Tatiana Méndez, specialist in Japanese Kobido facial lifting and skin treatments.

Every session is designed to release tension, restore balance, and bring your body and skin back to their natural vitality. We welcome you to a calm, carefully designed space — warm light, gentle aromas, and our full attention on you.
